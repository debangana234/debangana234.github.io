Bio

I am pursuing PhD studies in Computational Biology at University Hospital, RWTH Aachen University. I am a part of the Institute of Computational Biomedicine and Disease Modelling, which collaborates with the Joint Research Computation group and Centre for Computational Life Sciences at RWTH Aachen.

My PhD project focuses on developing mathematical and data-driven computational models for identifying epigenetic networks in blood cancers. The project is co-supervised by Prof. Dr. Thomas Stiehl and Prof. Dr. Wolfgang Wagner at the Institute of Stem Cell Biology.

Research

I enjoy formulating scientific questions at the intersection of life science and informatics and solving them. My current work includes statistical analysis of methylation datasets, mathematical modelling, and data-drive modelling using deep neural networks to answer questions in context of Leukemia.

Background

  • I completed B.Tech in Information Technology from Institute of Engineering and Management, West Bengal University of Technology.
  • I worked as a Database Developer in Tata Consultancy Services from 2016-2018. During my tenure as a Database Developer, I handled large complex datasets, which fuelled my interest in Biomedical Data Science.
  • Taking a step towards pursuing my interest, I completed my Master in Bioinformatics from Saarland University.
  • My Master thesis, titled Bioinformatic Analysis of Blood DNA Methylomes to Identify Prodromal Biomarkers Linked to Parkinson's Disease, was supervised by Prof. Dr. Joern Walter and Prof. Dr. Fabian Mueller and was submitted in March 2025.
  • During the course of my Master studies, I worked as a Database Engineer at Eurodata AG from 2022-2025. I come with a blend of industry and research experience.
